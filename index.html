I started in game development, where state, latency, and failure are immediately visible to the person on the other side of the screen. Since co-founding Imagicast in 2021, I’ve expanded from gameplay systems into infrastructure, languages, and developer tools.

I like understanding where state lives, what happens when a dependency disappears, how a change gets rolled back, and whether the next engineer can tell what the system is doing.
